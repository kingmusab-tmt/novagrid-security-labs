# Rubric — Episode 32 capstone (100 pts)

| Area | What "good" looks like | Pts |
|------|------------------------|-----|
| Master timeline | Accurate, chronological, every event has an evidence reference; T0 justified | 15 |
| Kill-chain map | Every stage mapped to ATT&CK technique + evidence + control/gap + mitigation | 20 |
| Analysis quality | Detection→containment→eradication→recovery is coherent and evidence-backed | 20 |
| Earliest-stop insight | Identifies the single most effective missing control, well defended | 10 |
| Kill-chain diagram | Clear diagram showing the path and where controls caught/failed | 10 |
| Executive brief | One page, plain language, business framing, a single clear ask | 15 |
| Lessons & remediation | Gaps turned into prioritised, owned, dated actions | 5 |
| Evidence & honesty | Claims cite real evidence files; framed honestly as a lab | 5 |

**Evidence required in `evidence/`:** the master timeline, the ATT&CK kill-chain table, the
diagram, the full case file, and the one-page executive brief. A case file that asserts
findings with no evidence reference caps Analysis quality — in a real IR, unsupported claims
are worthless.
