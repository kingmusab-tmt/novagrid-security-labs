# Instructor Answer Key — Episode 24
- A good hunt is hypothesis-driven and ATT&CK-grounded, not "poke around". It must be testable ("if X then Y in Z").
- Reward checking for ABSENCE of evidence and multi-source corroboration (guards against confirmation bias).
- Strongest outcome: a confirmed technique the alerts missed, converted into an automated detection (Ep20) or, if not automatable, a defined recurring hunt.
- Verification: (1) hypothesis + technique; (2) what the data showed + bias control; (3) the resulting detection or recurring-hunt justification.
