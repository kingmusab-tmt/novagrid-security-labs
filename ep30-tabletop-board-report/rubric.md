# Rubric — Episode 30 (100 pts)

| Area | What "good" looks like | Pts |
|------|------------------------|-----|
| Exercise design | Clear objectives, roles, scope, blameless ground rules | 10 |
| Decision log | A real decision + owner + time target captured for every inject | 20 |
| Gap quality | Gaps are specific, about the *plan*, ranked by business risk | 20 |
| Remediation | Actions are prioritised, **owned**, and dated | 15 |
| Board brief — clarity | One page, plain language, no jargon, business framing | 20 |
| Board brief — the ask | A single, specific decision the board can actually give | 10 |
| Reflection/tone | Blameless, honest, leadership-appropriate | 5 |

**Evidence required in `evidence/`:** the inject decision log, the ranked gap list, the
AAR, and the board brief. A board brief longer than one page or full of jargon caps that area.
