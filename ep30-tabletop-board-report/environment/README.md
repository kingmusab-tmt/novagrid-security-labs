# Environment — Episode 30 (no tools)

This is a **discussion-based** exercise. Nothing to install.

## Run it one of two ways
- **Solo:** you facilitate and reason through every inject, recording the decision, the
  owner, and the gap. Good for a portfolio piece — you still produce both documents.
- **Group:** assign the roles (SOC Lead, Sysadmin, CFO, Compliance, Facilitator) and work
  the injects as a 60–90 minute session. Richer, and closer to the real thing.

## Materials
- `injects.md` — the scenario and the timed injects to read out, one at a time.
- `../deliverable-template.md` — the after-action report + board brief to fill in.
- Keep your running notes (decisions/owners/gaps) in `../evidence/`.

## Facilitation ground rules
- **Blameless** — you're testing the plan, not people.
- **Time-box** each inject (≈5–8 min of decision-making).
- Reveal injects **one at a time**; don't let the group skip ahead.
- Capture every "we'd have to check who does that" — those are your gaps.
