# Episode 18 — Credential Stuffing on Customer Accounts

**Season 2 · Under attack · difficulty: intermediate**
**Security+ SY0-701:** 2.0 Threats, Vulnerabilities & Mitigations (password attacks)

Customers are reporting locked accounts and odd logins. It looks like credential stuffing —
attackers replaying username/password pairs leaked from *other* breaches. Detect it in the
logs and design the mitigations. Artifact: a detection + mitigation write-up.
