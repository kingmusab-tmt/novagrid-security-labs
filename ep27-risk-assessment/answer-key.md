# Instructor Answer Key — Episode 27
- SLE = Asset Value × Exposure Factor; ALE = SLE × ARO. Students must show working and use ALE to justify control spend (spend < ALE reduction).
- Inherent risk = before controls; residual risk = what remains after existing controls. Risk appetite = how much residual the org will tolerate.
- Four treatments: Mitigate (add controls), Transfer (insurance/outsource), Avoid (stop the activity), Accept (sign-off by the risk owner).
- Verification: (1) SLE/ARO/ALE computed; (2) inherent vs residual; (3) the four treatments w/ NovaGrid examples.
